## Simplifications

- The main table plays no chips; the Chips section, when present, adds them to `xp`.
- No Assistant Manager chip (2024-25).
- Each strategy's transfer policy is in its name: `xp`, `form`, `ownership` and `hindsight` make one transfer a week if it gains anything; `xp_use_banked` saves transfers for bigger gains; `xp_allow_hits` may also take one 4-point hit a week.
- No injury news: scorers only see past minutes.
- Starts at gameweek 5, so totals cover GW5 to the end of the season.
- No vice-captain: if the captain doesn't play, nobody is doubled.
- Transfers come from the app's own search, which shortlists 25 upgrades per squad slot before checking the budget.
